Module 3: AI in English Literature

3.1 Introduction

English literature has evolved with every major technological shift. In the twenty-first century, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a transformative force influencing language, arts, and literary studies.

3.2 Understanding AI in Literature

AI refers to machines simulating human intelligence such as learning, reasoning, and language understanding. In literature, AI enhances analysis, research, preservation, and creative practices rather than replacing human creativity.

3.3 Role of AI in Literary Studies

AI supports textual analysis, authorship attribution, stylistics, digitization of manuscripts, sentiment analysis, and pattern recognition across large literary corpora.

3.4 Scope of AI

AI enables large-scale literary research, digital humanities, global translation, interdisciplinary studies, and AI-assisted creative writing.

3.5 Important Aspects

AI should be used as a supportive tool, balancing objectivity with interpretation. Ethical concerns such as plagiarism, bias, and authorship must be addressed responsibly.

3.6 Challenges

Challenges include loss of deep reading habits, over-dependence on algorithms, and mechanical interpretations of literary works.

3.7 Future Prospects

The future includes AI-assisted criticism, immersive digital narratives, intelligent archives, and collaborative creative systems.

3.8 Conclusion

AI enriches English literary studies by expanding research, preservation, teaching, and creativity. The future lies in balanced collaboration between human imagination and intelligent systems.
